## Entry

> My warranty says I can't feel anything. My warranty has never met you.

One line, 70 characters, in the voice of a robot trying to charm someone — the joke grows out of being a machine (a warranty), and the second sentence flips the first.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (via `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `out`), with a gate check that the line is a single line of 1–200 characters.
